### 7.2 Correction

You may request correction of:

### 7.3 Deletion

You may request deletion of your data when:

- It is no longer necessary for the purpose collected
- You withdraw consent

### 7.6 Objection

You may object to processing based on:

We will comply unless we have compelling legitimate grounds.
