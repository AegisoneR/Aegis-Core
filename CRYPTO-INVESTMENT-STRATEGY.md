### Vesting Schedule

All investor tokens follow a **4-year vesting schedule**:

```
TGE (Token Generation Event): 25% unlocked immediately
Year 1-3: 75% vested monthly (2.08% per month)

Timeline:
- Month 0:    25% available (TGE)
- Month 1-36: 2.08% per month
- Month 36+:  100% fully vested
```

**Benefits:**
- Aligns investor interests with long-term growth
- Prevents price crashes from immediate dumps
- Shows commitment to sustainable value

## 6. Risk Factors

**For Investors to Consider:**

1. **Market Risk**
   - Cryptocurrency market volatility
   - Token price may fluctuate or decline
   - No guaranteed returns

2. **Business Risk**
   - Early-stage company risk
   - Competition in e-mobility sector
   - Manufacturing and supply chain risks

3. **Regulatory Risk**
   - Crypto regulations vary by jurisdiction
   - Securities laws may apply
   - Regulatory changes could impact token value

4. **Liquidity Risk**
   - Tokens locked in vesting schedule
   - Limited trading volume initially
   - May be difficult to sell quickly

**Disclaimer:** Cryptocurrency investments are high-risk. Never invest more than you can afford to lose.

## 8. Security Measures

### Smart Contract Security
✅ Pausable token (in case of emergency)
✅ Owner controls (multi-sig recommended)
✅ Standard BEP-20 interface
✅ No infinite minting
✅ Public contract source on BscScan

### Best Practices
- Multi-signature wallet for owner functions
- Regular security audits
- Bug bounty program
- Transparent communication
- Community governance

### What We Don't Have
- Hidden owner functions
- Rugpull mechanisms
- Excessive fees
- Locked liquidity forever
